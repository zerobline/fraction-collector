- English: *10-position acrylic test-tube rack*, *16 mm OD × 120 mm glass test tubes*, *smooth rim*, *single row*.
- The original purchase screenshot identified the 100-pack of 120 × Ø16 mm tubes as **32161**, but the current product page uses configurable variants; verify the SKU at checkout rather than using it as the sole identifier.

### Substitutes

A substitute rack must have **10 equally spaced centers at 27 mm** (or you must change the CAD pitch), enough clearance for its full-length strip, and the right assembled elevation. A substitute tube must be **Ø16 mm outside** with enough internal clearance for a **Ø13.8 mm locating sleeve** and an appropriate rim for a **Ø17 mm shoulder**.

Do **not** substitute the visually similar 160 × 16 mm tube without checking total installed height; the rack is often sold bundled with that longer tube size.

### Compatibility checklist

- [ ] Rack row: 10 holes in one line
- [ ] Rack hole center pitch measured; expected ~27 mm
- [ ] Each rack hole allows Ø16 mm tubes
- [ ] Actual tube mouth I.D. clears Ø13.8 mm insertion sleeve, including tolerances
- [ ] Ø17 mm shoulder contacts the rim without rocking or interference
- [ ] Tube/rack assembly fits the available vertical space
- [ ] Printing material and cleaning method are appropriate to the actual fluid
